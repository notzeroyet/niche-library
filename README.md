# Niche Library

Free niche ideas for print on demand shirts and products. Mix more than 1,300 niches by what people do, who
they are and what they love, then open the Etsy Check on any idea before you design it.

**Use it online if you're not tech savvy:** https://notzeroyet.com/niche-library/

**Video tutorial:** [Watch the Niche Library walkthrough on YouTube](https://youtu.be/vztem4pxzYQ)

## Open your own copy

The whole tool is one file, `index.html`. There's nothing to install and no accounts necessary.

1. On this page, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip it. On a Mac, double click the ZIP file. On Windows, right click it and choose **Extract All**.
3. Open the unzipped folder and double click `index.html`. It opens in your web browser.

It works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, on Mac, Windows, Linux and Chromebook. On a Chromebook, open
the file from the Files app. On a phone, use the website instead, since phones don't open files from a ZIP
very well.

The first time you open it you need an internet connection so the fonts can load. After that the tool runs
in your browser on your own computer.

## How to use it

1. Pick how specific you want each idea: **1 Niche**, **2 Niches** or **3 Niches**.
2. Narrow it down if you like: who they are, what they do, what they love, or an occasion.
3. Press **New Ideas**. Leave **Dig Deeper** on to favor the less common niches.
4. Press **Etsy Check** on any idea to further your research, and **Save** the ones you like.
5. Press **Copy List** and paste it into the [Idea Spinner](https://notzeroyet.com/idea-spinner/) to turn
   your niche ideas list into potential POD phrases.

You can also switch to **Browse the Library** to search every niche by name or type.

These are ideas for you to play with. Do your own research before you design.

## Your data

Your saved list stays in your own browser, on your own computer. There's no account, and the downloaded copy
sends nothing anywhere. (The website version counts visits with Google Analytics; the downloaded copy doesn't.)
